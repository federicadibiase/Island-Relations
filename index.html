Narrating the Island/s

Listening to Islanders — how heritage, migration, and identity are lived on islands, large and small.

Three thematic zones

The exhibition moves through three bodies of testimony, each rooted in a different island reality.

Heritage digitisation
in Cyprus

Voices from those documenting, digitising, and safeguarding Cyprus's cultural heritage — where the small islands around the coast have no institutions of their own, and the work concentrates on the main island.

Migration dynamics

Testimonies on movement, arrival, and settlement — the ways island life is shaped by who comes, who leaves, and who stays.

Community identity
on Møn, Denmark

Stories of belonging on a small Danish island, where community identity is negotiated at a different scale.

52 voices

The exhibition is built entirely from interview material — fifty-two quotes, drawn directly from the people who live these realities.

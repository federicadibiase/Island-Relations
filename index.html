01 — 03

Three projects

The exhibition connects three projects and presents a critical reflection on island research.

01

Digital Storytelling in Cultural Heritage Institutions
of Small Mediterranean Islands

Critically analyses the digital portfolios and challenges of small, remote cultural heritage institutions in the central and eastern Mediterranean, ultimately aiming to empower cultural heritage officers through capacity-building initiatives to create engaging and impactful stories through digital technologies.

02

Making Space: Migration and Socio-Spatial Relations in Cyprus

Addresses the role of Cyprus as both a bridge to and external border of Europe as increasing numbers of migrants face strained reception systems and harsh conditions yet navigate and shape their environments through space-making practices in the reception centres, neighbourhoods and public spaces of Cypriot cities.

03

Dark Sky Discourse: Tools for Planetary Heritage Futures

Investigates how eco-discourse around designated European 'dark sky' island communities combating light pollution exemplifies a 'planetary' approach to heritage for human and non-human wellbeing and sustainable tourism development, both challenging and complementing conventional global heritage concepts and practices.
